# Design System: O/L ICT Practice

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Daylight Glass"**

A student practising for the exam that decides their A/L stream sits in a room that feels good to be in. The page is a fixed field of soft daylight (peach top-left, sky top-right, lavender low right, a breath of mint bottom-left) and every piece of content is a sheet of milk glass laid over it: translucent white, a lit rim along its edge, a long violet-tinted shadow, a big 28px corner. The world refuses both the paper-and-serif exam sheet and the dense SaaS grid. It is airy, light-weight in its type, and generous in its spacing, so a tired fifteen-year-old on a mid-range Android can read for forty minutes without strain.

Colour does two jobs only: identity (the pastel family, each tint with its own ink) and data (a violet/teal series pair, three meter ramps). The live thing on any screen, whether the primary action, the current section or the selected option, is a solid ink pill: black by day, chalk white at night. Correctness carries semantic colour but never travels on colour alone; there is always an icon and a word beside it. The measurement is honest in its shapes as well as its numbers: an unmeasured topic draws a dashed empty outline, an early estimate draws a meter with a hatched uncertainty tail, and a measured reading draws a solid gradient fill.

Night is a first-class theme rather than an inversion: the same wash rendered as a low aurora over deep indigo, glass at five percent white, and the ink pill turning to chalk. A trace of fractal grain (3% by day, 4.5% at night) sits over everything so the large gradients never band on an 8-bit phone panel.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Fixed four-point pastel wash behind everything, painted once, never scrolled.
- Translucent milk-glass sheets with lit rims and violet-tinted ambient shadows; one opaque sheet per screen.
- Ink pill as the sole accent; colour is spent on the pastel identity family and on data.
- Lexend at light weights for display, regular for reading; JetBrains Mono for syllabus data.
- Pills for controls, 28px sheets, 20px inner panels, 16px tiles.
- Three meter states that cannot be mistaken for each other.
- Floating island chrome: tab island, icon rail, bottom pill nav.

## Colors

A near-white daylight field tinted at its corners, a single ink accent, four pastel tints that each bring their own ink, and a small data and semantic set that never competes with the ink.

### Primary
- **Ink Pill** (`accent`, chalk `ink-night` at night): the one thing to press. Primary buttons, the active nav tab, the active rail item, the selected answer key, icon tiles, the brand tile, tooltips, the resume CTA. Hover lifts to `accent-hover`. Text on it is `accent-ink`.
- **Violet Focus** (`focus` / `focus-night`): the single focus ring and the text caret. It is violet rather than ink because an ink ring disappears on the ink pill.

### Secondary
- **The Pastel Family**: Lilac (`lilac` with `lilac-ink`), Sky (`sky` with `sky-ink`), Mint (`mint` with `mint-ink`), Peach (`peach` with `peach-ink`). Whole sheets (the recommendation card, the weak-topic tiles), icon tiles in a list, the avatar, the exam-tip panel, and text selection (lilac). Text on a tint is drawn from that tint's ink and never greyed. At night the tints become low-alpha glows of the same hue and the inks become pale.

### Tertiary
- **Data Pair** (`series-1` violet, `series-2` teal; night `series-1-night`, `series-2-night`): the weekly chart's answered and correct series. They are validated as a pair for colour-blind separation in both themes and are always named in a legend.
- **Meter Ramps**: plain progress runs `meter-from` mint into `meter-to` sky; strength (75% and above) runs `meter-high-from` into the teal `meter-high-to`; what needs work (below 40%) runs apricot `meter-low-from` into `meter-low-to`. The printed value beside a meter is the reading; the ramp is the second signal.
- **Past-Paper Brass** (`brass` on `brass-soft`): provenance only. A warm apricot that sits apart from the ink so "this came from a real paper" never reads as "do this next". It also marks the official-key seal.
- **Correctness**: `correct` / `correct-soft`, `wrong` / `wrong-soft`, `caution` / `caution-soft`. Always with an icon and a word.

### Neutral
- **Daylight Wash** (`wash-peach`, `wash-sky`, `wash-lavender`, `wash-mint` over a `wash-base-top` to `wash-base-bottom` vertical; the `-night` stops at night): four radial glows at 0%/0%, 100%/0%, 92%/72%, 4%/100%, each fading to transparent by 70%. Fixed to the viewport on `body::before`.
- **Canvas** (`canvas` / `canvas-night`): the solid fallback beneath the wash.
- **Milk Glass** (`surface` / `surface-night`): default sheet fill, translucent over the wash.
- **Opaque Sheet** (`raised` / `raised-night`): the one sheet that is the subject of its screen, secondary buttons, tooltips, unselected answer options.
- **Sunken** (`sunken` / `sunken-night`): wells. Fields, meter tracks, stat cells, neutral pills, hover grounds, inline code.
- **Ink, Muted, Faint** (`ink`, `ink-muted`, `ink-faint` and night): three weights of text presence. Faint is for axis ticks, placeholders and disabled text only.
- **Hairlines** (`line` structural, `line-soft` whisper, `rim` the lit edge of glass).

### Named Rules
**The Ink Is The Accent Rule.** The ink pill marks the live thing, and a viewport gets one primary. A second primary turns the first into decoration. When a more urgent action exists (an unfinished session), the recommendation card's buttons drop to secondary white pills.

**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Right, wrong, trend direction, provenance and mastery tone are each carried by a mark and a word first; colour is the third signal. This is binding product truth.

**The Tint Carries Its Ink Rule.** A pastel goes on the sheet, never on a panel inside a white sheet, and text on it uses that tint's own ink.

**The Provenance Is Not Action Rule.** Past-paper badges are brass, solid and sealed; model questions are dashed, plain and pencilled. Never ink for provenance.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Lexend (variable, self-hosted via next/font; fallback ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Lexend
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono 400/500 (fallback ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular) for syllabus data: binary, hex, cell references, formulas, Pascal, HTML, topic codes.

**Character:** One airy geometric does every voice. Drawn to reduce visual stress, it reads as calm at 300 weight and display sizes and stays legible for long reading at 15 to 18px. The mono is not decoration; it is how a transcription error stays visible.

### Hierarchy
The ramp in use: 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 16 / 17 / 18 / 20 / 22 / 28 / 30 / 38 / 42 / 56 / 72px.
- **Display hero** (300, 42px rising to 72px, 1.04, -0.04em): the landing page's hero line only.
- **Display** (300, 38px rising to 56px, 1.06, -0.04em): a page's own name, set directly on the wash ("Hello, Nimali"). The first thing on the screen.
- **Headline** (300, 30px rising to 42px, 1.12, -0.035em): section headings on long-form marketing pages.
- **Numeral** (300, 28 / 42 / 56px, line-height 1, -0.04em, tabular): display figures (mastery percentage, stat cells, recommendation count), set in reserved digit slots so figures down a column end on the same vertical. A trailing `%` drops to 22px light muted.
- **Title** (400, 20px rising to 22px, 1.2, -0.025em): sheet titles and in-page section heads, with a 13px muted note underneath and an optional count pill beside.
- **Question** (400, 18px, 1.6, -0.01em): question stems. Never below 17px.
- **Lead** (400, 17px, 1.65): the intro paragraph under a page title on marketing, legal and review pages.
- **Option** (400, 16px, 1.5): answer option text. Also the document base size.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.6): the workhorse; explanations, list rows, card prose. Measure capped by `max-w-prose` or 40 to 68ch.
- **Control** (500, 15px; 13px small, 16px large; line-height 1, -0.01em): button labels.
- **Nav** (400, 14px; 500 when active): tab island labels.
- **Label** (400, 13px, 1.4, sentence case, muted, untracked): captions that name a quantity or a field, and sheet notes.
- **Caption** (500, 12px, line-height 1): pills, badges, axis ticks, bottom-nav labels.

### Named Rules
**The Light Display Rule.** Anything 28px and up is weight 300 with tight negative tracking; anything under 18px stays at 400 or 500. Headings default to 400, never bold.

**The Question Floor Rule.** Question text is 18px and never goes below 17px. It is the one size taken from a person rather than a scale.

**The Tabular Body Rule.** The body runs `tabular-nums` everywhere, so counts and scores line up without per-element opt-in.

**The Caption Not Kicker Rule.** The 13px label names a quantity or a field (a stat cell's "Answered", a figure caption, a table header). It never stands above a heading as a kicker, and it is never uppercase or tracked.

## Layout

The student frame is a floating composition rather than a bordered app shell. A sticky header 80px tall (96px from 768px) holds three islands: the brand tile left, the four-tab pill island centred, and the student's corner right. Behind them a strip of backdrop blur fades out downward through a mask, so content scrolling under the chrome softens instead of colliding. From 768px a slim icon rail floats vertically centred at the left edge (20px, 28px from 1024px) for less-visited places. On phones the tabs move to a floating bottom pill bar clear of the edge (12px or the safe-area inset).

Content runs in a single column of stacked rows with a 24px gap (32px from 640px). Desktop rows split 5fr / 7fr (`minmax(0,5fr) minmax(0,7fr)`) with a 20px gap (24px from 1024px): recommendation beside the week chart, list beside the standing card. The weak-topic row is a 1 / 2 / 4 column grid of standalone tinted sheets. Sheets fill their grid row height so neighbours end on the same line, except where stretching would leave a hollow middle. Sheet padding is 20px on phones and 28px from 640px; list rows use the same horizontal padding so their text lines up under the title.

Marketing pages centre at `max-w-6xl`; the session runner centres at `max-w-3xl` with a floating title island on top and a floating action island pinned to the bottom. Breakpoints are Tailwind defaults (640 / 768 / 1024 / 1536).

**The 48px Floor Rule.** Every control a thumb reaches on a student surface is at least 48px (`spacing.touch`). Large buttons and bottom-nav items are 56px; answer options are at least 64px. The 36px small button exists for mouse-driven admin chrome only.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is ambient light, not structure. Sheets float in the wash on long, diffused, violet-tinted shadows (`shade` is `rgba(70, 62, 140, …)` by day, black at night) combined with an inset 1px rim that reads as the lit edge of glass. There are no hard or offset shadows. Opacity is the second axis: glass is translucent, the subject sheet is opaque, wells are sunken.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hairline** (`0 0 0 1px line-soft`): a whisper outline.
- **Raised** (`0 1px 2px 0 shade-weak, 0 8px 24px -12px shade`): secondary buttons at rest, empty-state chips.
- **Float** (`0 2px 6px -2px shade-weak, 0 24px 48px -22px shade, inset 0 1px 0 0 sheen`): hover lift, tooltips, avatar, skip link.
- **Lifted** (`0 4px 12px -4px shade-weak, 0 40px 80px -32px shade-strong, inset 0 1px 0 0 sheen`): the one opaque subject sheet.
- **Glass** (`inset 0 0 0 1px rim, 0 1px 2px 0 shade-weak, 0 22px 48px -28px shade`): every milk-glass sheet, tinted sheet and island.
- **Ink pill** (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.14), 0 10px 24px -10px shade-strong`, deepening to `0 14px 30px -10px` on hover): primary buttons and the brand tile.

### Named Rules
**The Painted-Once Rule.** The wash and the grain are `position: fixed` and never repaint on scroll. Scrolling sheets are translucent, never blurred. Backdrop blur (18px, saturate 1.4) is only allowed on fixed or sticky chrome (nav islands, session bars, the header fade strip, modal scrims), where it composites once.

**The One Opaque Sheet Rule.** A screen gets one `glass-raised` sheet, the card that is its subject (the question card in a session, the submit dialog). Everything else is glass.

## Shapes

Big, soft, nested radii. Controls are full pills (9999px). Sheets have a 28px corner from 640px and 24px on phones, where a bigger curve would eat into a 360px column. Panels and answer options inside a sheet step down to 20px, inset stat cells and hoverable list rows to 18px, fields and icon tiles to 16px, answer keys and the small brand tile to 13px, so outer and inner curves never fight. Inline code is 6px. Borders are drawn as inset box-shadows rather than CSS borders, with two exceptions that carry meaning: the dashed outline of an unmeasured meter and the dashed outline of a model-question badge. Icons share a 20-unit viewBox with round caps and joins.

## Components

### Buttons
Objects rather than rectangles: they press, their shadows change with state, and a primary CTA carries its arrow in its own disc.
- **Shape:** full pill (9999px); the icon button is a 48px square at 16px radius.
- **Primary:** ink fill with accent-ink text, 15px medium, 48px tall with 24px side padding (56px and 28px at large).
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** background shifts to `accent-hover` and the shadow deepens over 200ms on `ease-settle`; press scales to 0.98; focus is the shared 2px violet outline at 2px offset.
- **Affordance disc:** a 32px circle at 15% of the text colour holding the arrow, flush to the right padding, nudging 2px right on hover.
- **Secondary:** opaque white pill with an inset `line` hairline and the Raised shadow; on hover the hairline darkens to `accent-line` and the shadow goes to Float.
- **Ghost:** muted text, sunken ground on hover.
- **Danger:** wrong-soft fill with an inset wrong outline, flooding to solid `wrong` on hover.
- **Disabled:** sunken ground, faint text, a `line` hairline; it reads as inert rather than as a hole.

### Chips (Pills)
- **Style:** the one badge shape. Full pill, 12px medium, 6px by 12px, an icon at 14px then the word. Tones: neutral (sunken/muted), glass (raised with hairline), accent (ink), correct, wrong, caution, brass.
- **Provenance:** Past paper is a brass pill with a seal and a mono paper reference; Model question is a transparent pill with a dashed faint outline and a pencil. They differ on mark, ground, outline and wording at once.
- **Count pill:** sits beside a sheet title, 13px medium on sunken.

### Cards / Containers (Sheets)
- **Corner Style:** 28px (24px on phones).
- **Background:** glass by default; opaque raised for the screen's subject; quiet (half-strength glass with a rim) for a recessive pane; lilac, sky, mint or peach for a sheet that is itself an identity card.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Glass, or Lifted for the raised sheet (see Elevation).
- **Border:** none; the rim is an inset shadow.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px phones, 28px from 640px; header 20/24px top, body 24/28px bottom.
- **Header:** title with its note underneath like a figure caption, never a bordered strip.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** a well, not a raised box. 52px tall, 16px radius, sunken ground, inset `line-soft` hairline, 16px text, 14px medium label above.
- **Focus:** ground turns opaque `raised` and the hairline darkens to `accent-line`, plus the global violet outline.
- **Error:** three signals at once: wrong-soft ground, inset wrong outline, and an alert icon beside 13px medium wrong text, linked by `aria-describedby`.

### Navigation
- **Tab island (768px and up):** a pill group on island glass, 6px inner padding; tabs are 48px pills with 20px side padding, 14px muted text, sunken on hover; the current tab is the ink pill with a soft drop shadow and `aria-current`. A count rides inside as a small pill (wrong-soft, or 20% accent-ink when active).
- **Icon rail (768px and up):** a vertical island of 48px, 16px-radius icon cells with an ink tooltip on hover and focus; a 24px hairline separates sign-out.
- **Bottom bar (phones):** a floating island pill, four 56px items with a 20px icon over a 12px label; the current one fills ink.
- **Chrome disappears inside a session.** The runner has its own floating title island (56px, full pill) and a 28px-radius action island at the bottom.

### Answer Option (Signature)
A 64px-minimum, 20px-radius row: a 40px, 13px-radius key holding the option number, then 16px option text. Unselected is opaque raised with a `line` hairline, sunken on hover. Selected is `accent-soft` with a 1.5px ink inset and an ink key. After answering, the right option turns correct-soft with a correct outline, a check in a solid correct key and the word "Correct"; the student's wrong pick turns wrong-soft with a cross and "Your answer"; the rest drop to 55% opacity. The explanation panel settles in (320ms) and the verdict mark lands 140ms later on the same curve, so it reads as one gesture in two parts.

### Meters (Signature)
Three states that cannot be mistaken for one another. **Unmeasured:** an 8px dashed outline with nothing inside, and no percentage anywhere. **Early estimate:** a sunken track, the gradient fill at 70% opacity, and a 135-degree hatched tail in `ink-faint` running to the right edge for the range still open, with the word "estimate" by the value. **Measured:** a solid gradient fill: high ramp at 75% and above, plain ramp from 40%, low ramp below. Width transitions over 500ms.

### Weekly Chart (Signature)
Server-rendered SVG of two overlapping smoothed areas, answered (`series-1`) and correct (`series-2`), each filled with fine 1.5px vertical rules on a 5px pitch under a top-to-bottom fade mask, with 2px non-scaling strokes and `line-soft` gridlines. The y-axis sits on the right in 12px faint tabular figures. Each day's count is printed under its label, because a phone has no hover. The peak gets a ringed marker and an ink callout. It draws in left to right once (900ms clip).

### Numeral
Display figures at 300 weight in reserved digit slots (0.58em per slot, flush right), so 7 and 148 end on the same vertical down a column. `slots=0` reserves nothing, for a figure that sits tight against its word.

### Motion
One curve, `ease-settle` (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`), with `ease-exit` for leaving. Durations are short for the target phone: 200ms state changes, 320ms reveal, 260ms verdict, 520ms page-in stagger at 50ms steps capped at 200ms, 900ms chart draw. Transform and opacity only. Every authored animation is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every surface as a sheet over the fixed wash: glass by default, one opaque raised sheet per screen.
- **Do** give a viewport exactly one ink-pill primary; drop competing actions to secondary white pills.
- **Do** pair every correctness, trend and provenance colour with an icon and a word.
- **Do** draw unmeasured as a dashed empty outline and early estimates with a hatched tail; show no percentage without evidence.
- **Do** set page titles and figures in Lexend 300 with tight negative tracking, and keep question text at 18px.
- **Do** set syllabus data (binary, hex, cell references, code, topic codes) in JetBrains Mono.
- **Do** step radii down as you nest: 28 sheet, 20 panel, 18 cell, 16 tile, 13 key.
- **Do** keep student-facing touch targets at 48px or more, 56px for primary navigation and large CTAs.
- **Do** carry each pastel tint with its own ink, and apply the tint to the whole sheet.
- **Do** design night as its own theme: aurora wash over indigo, 5% glass, chalk pills.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put a backdrop blur on anything that scrolls; blur belongs only to fixed or sticky chrome.
- **Don't** use colour as the only carrier of right, wrong, trend or provenance.
- **Don't** use ink or the accent for provenance; past paper is brass, model is dashed.
- **Don't** place a pastel panel inside a white sheet; one surface, not two.
- **Don't** use hard or offset shadows, or grey drop shadows; depth is violet-tinted ambient light.
- **Don't** stack an uppercase or tracked label above a heading as a kicker.
- **Don't** add gamification: no confetti, celebration bounce, flame or reward motifs, or animation triggered by a right answer.
- **Don't** introduce colours outside the named tokens; the default Tailwind palette is switched off.
- **Don't** exceed 900ms on any authored animation, or keyframe anything other than transform, opacity or clip; state changes are colour and shadow transitions of 200ms.
